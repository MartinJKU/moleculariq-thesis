Metrics & plots
pass@1 / pass@k (Chen et al.) · accuracy · validity vs correctness · answer-diversity diagnostics · crossover report.
Why it's a clean RL signal: the same symbolic verifier scores every completion exactly, so the training reward and the benchmark are the same oracle — no learned reward model, no LLM judge, and no train/test leakage. pass@k then separates RL sharpening from genuine capability expansion.
